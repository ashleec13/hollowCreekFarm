Our Family Story

For over three generations, Hollow Creek Farm has been a family-owned destination where visitors can enjoy the beauty of the countryside and the traditions of each season. Since 1962, the Calloway family has welcomed guests to explore 140 acres of farmland featuring fresh produce, seasonal attractions, and a warm, family-friendly atmosphere where lasting memories are made.
